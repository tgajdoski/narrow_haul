#### Nomination Description [1000] (963)
Narrow Haul is a physics cave flyer in the line of Thrust and Gravitar, rebuilt for touch. You fly a small cargo tug through narrow caves, hook a heavy pod on a line and land both on the pad while the pod swings behind you. Real physics, a thrust button and a turn stick, and every drop of fuel counts.

There are 68 hand-built missions: 60 across seven worlds (low gravity, mine machinery, ice and wind, lava, gravity wells and zero-g, and a fortress that shoots back) plus 8 long multi-leg Expeditions. Six ships fly differently. The story plays out by radio: the jump gate has gone dark, and you haul rations, vaccine and coolant to cut-off settlements.

It's made by one developer. His kids sign off every build and are credited on the first screen as "Approved by". The whole game plays offline, ads are light, and the Full Game is a single purchase. There's a Daily Challenge, up to 3 stars per mission, a route guide and demo flights for when you're stuck.

## Google Play

Google has two forms. Neither is in Play Console; both are support contact
forms.

### 1. Indie Games Corner ("Indie Games / Up-and-Coming Apps Featuring Request")

Form: https://support.google.com/googleplay/contact/indie_corners

- **Eligibility:** a team of 1–15, made "in the indie spirit", and an **average
  rating ≥ 3.8**. Newly released games are featured 1–2 months after release,
  if picked. Wait until the Play page shows a rating.
- **No text field for a pitch.** The form is checkboxes and facts. Fill it in
  like this:

### 2. Featuring Nomination form (general)

Form: https://support.google.com/googleplay/contact/featuring_review

- Games need a **rating ≥ 3.8**. Submit ≥ 3 weeks ahead (the page also says 8
  weeks for a new launch). A new game must launch within 120 days of the
  featuring date.
- **This form doesn't take update requests for games.** Google says to use
  Promotional Content for updates (`in_app_events.md`). Narrow Haul launched
  on Play on 2026-10-20, so the only fit is a "new launch" request with a date
  before about 2027-02-17 (120 days). After that, use the Indie Corner form and
  promotional content.
- Rule: "Localization is required in target markets. Japan and Korea targeting
  requires Japanese or Korean localization." A localised store listing may not
  be enough when the game itself is English only. Pick English-speaking
  regions here, or ask about it.
- Free-text answers (no limits stated):
  - **Why featuring matters:** Narrow Haul is a one-person indie game with no ad budget. A feature is how a niche genre (Thrust-style cave flying) finds its players.
  - **Marketing / UA plan:** organic only so far (press kit, short gameplay clips, Reddit). Apple Ads and Google Ads trial credit planned, no paid budget.
  - **Monetization:** $0 upfront + IAP + ads (light interstitials, optional rewarded ads; one Full Game purchase removes the interstitials).
  - **Why it could succeed:** the nomination 1 description above fits here word for word.
  - **Pitch materials:** https://zafrk.com/narrow-haul/press
