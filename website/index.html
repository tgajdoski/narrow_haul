Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems: we read every message.

Common questions

I bought Remove Ads or the Supporter Pack on a new phone. How do I get it back?
Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Purchases are tied to your store account, not to the device.
Does Remove Ads remove every ad?
It removes the ads shown between missions. Optional "watch an ad" rewards (continue after a crash, double coins, try a cosmetic) stay available, because you only see them when you choose to.
How do I change my ad consent or tracking choice?
Players in the EU, UK and Switzerland can open Settings → Privacy options in the game at any time. On iPhone you can also change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
Is my progress saved in the cloud?
No. Stars, ranks, coins and cosmetics are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Purchases can always be restored from the store.
How do I delete my data?
Uninstall the game. We keep no account or server copy of your progress. For data held by Google's ad services, see the privacy policy.
The ship keeps crashing as soon as I touch the controls.
The ship waits on its pad until your first input. Use short, gentle thrust bursts: gravity is weak but momentum is not. Left-handed controls and vibration settings are in Settings.

Privacy Policy

Narrow Haul · Last updated:

In short: the game has no accounts and keeps your progress on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID with your consent where the law requires it. Crash reports and anonymous gameplay statistics (no personal details) help us fix bugs and balance levels. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

1. Who we are

Narrow Haul ("the game") is published by . Contact us at for any privacy question or request.

2. What the game stores on your device

Your progress (stars, best times, pilot rank and XP, coins, unlocked cosmetics, tow gear and handling kits, carried weapon ammo, achievements, daily challenge and contract state) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, steering and camera, minimap) are saved locally on your device. Your save itself stays on your device and is not sent to us (anonymous gameplay statistics are described in section 6). Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The game has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA),
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system and language,
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks).

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. On iOS, the game asks for permission before using the IDFA for tracking; if you decline, ads are not personalized. You can also reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases

Optional purchases (Remove Ads, Supporter Pack, and the Demolition Kit and Arsenal Crate ammo packs) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The store tells the game which products you own so they can be unlocked and restored. Ammo packs are delivered to your device's Armory once per purchase; like other in-game items they can't be restored after the game is deleted.

5. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the game crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends a crash report through Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the bug. A report contains the error and where in the code it happened, the device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier. It does not contain your name, contacts, location or advertising ID, and it is not used for advertising.

6. Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see which levels are too hard, where players get stuck and whether the game runs well, the game sends anonymous usage events through Google Analytics for Firebase. They include:

  • game events: a level started (with the ship, tow gear and mode), crashed (with the cause and the spot in the level), was abandoned or completed (stars, time, fuel left, attempt number), route guide and demo use, the tutorial finished, worlds unlocked, ranks, achievements, coins earned and spent, weapons used, supply crates and mystery salvage caches collected (and what they held), ads watched and purchase attempts with their result (never payment details),
  • three summary values: your pilot rank, your total stars and whether you have made a purchase,
  • app usage that Firebase collects on its own: first open, sessions and their length, app version, device model, operating system, language, country (derived from the IP address, which is not stored), and when the app is removed (Android),
  • a random app-instance identifier that is reset when you reinstall the game. Analytics does not collect your advertising ID.

These events are not linked to your name, email or any account, are not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and are not used to personalize ads. Where the law requires it, Google's consent signals from Settings → Privacy options limit how Firebase uses ad-related data. Google processes this data on our behalf; see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers for the game. Crash reports (section 5) and gameplay statistics (section 6) are used only to fix bugs and improve the game.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children (see section 9).

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through an ad interaction, contact us and we will help with the request to Google.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because the game keeps no personal data on our side, most requests are handled by uninstalling the game, changing Privacy options in the game, or contacting Google about ad data. We are happy to help: write to . You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
